## Annual view

Assumption BIZ-01: a year is 4 × "both" + 8 × "demand-swing" periods of 20 shifts. Payback = one-time cost ÷ annual profit gain before amortisation.

**Read with care (BIZ-02, COST-05):** every period here starts from an empty line, and stock options get their starting stock for free in every period.
